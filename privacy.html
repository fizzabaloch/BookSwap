Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 14, 2026

BookSwap ("BookSwap", "we", "us", or "our") is a technology marketplace that connects independent buyers and sellers of physical books, particularly used and pre-owned books, academic books, and competitive-examination preparation books.

This Privacy Policy explains how BookSwap collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the BookSwap Android application, website, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use BookSwap, we may collect information necessary to create accounts, operate the marketplace, process transactions, provide support, maintain security, and improve the service.

Account Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile phone number
  • Email address
  • Login or authentication information
  • Profile photograph
  • Age or date of birth where required
  • Account preferences and other information voluntarily provided by the user

Seller Verification Information

Users applying for seller verification or the paid seller subscription may be asked to provide information reasonably necessary for verification, including:

  • Full legal name
  • CNIC information
  • Contact information
  • Address
  • Profile photograph
  • Payment-account information
  • Other information reasonably necessary for verification

Verification information may be used to confirm seller information and help prevent fraud, abuse, and misuse of the platform. A verified badge does not mean that BookSwap guarantees a seller's honesty, book quality, authenticity, delivery performance, or future conduct.

Listing Information

When a user lists a book, BookSwap may collect and display:

  • Book title
  • Author
  • ISBN
  • Edition
  • Book condition
  • Price
  • Description
  • Photographs
  • Category and listing information
  • Listing date and status

Transaction and Delivery Information

For purchases and sales, BookSwap may collect:

  • Order and transaction information
  • Book and listing information
  • Purchase price
  • Seller commission and payout information
  • Payment reference information
  • Courier service name
  • Tracking number
  • Shipping date
  • Photograph or copy of the shipping receipt
  • Delivery status
  • Buyer confirmation of receipt

Chat and Communication Information

BookSwap provides communication features that may allow buyers and sellers to exchange:

  • Text messages
  • Photographs
  • Voice notes
  • Other information voluntarily shared through the platform

Users should not share passwords, OTPs, payment PINs, CNIC photographs, or unnecessary sensitive financial information through chat.

Ratings, Reviews, Reports, and Feedback

BookSwap may collect ratings, reviews, feedback, reports concerning users or listings, and complaints submitted through the platform or by email.

Technical and Usage Information

BookSwap may collect technical information reasonably necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the application, including:

  • Device information
  • Operating system
  • Application version
  • IP address
  • Log information
  • Crash and diagnostic information
  • Usage information
  • Security and fraud-prevention information

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts;
  • Provide buying and selling features;
  • Display and manage book listings;
  • Support wishlists and book discovery features;
  • Facilitate buyer-seller communication;
  • Process and record transactions;
  • Facilitate seller payouts;
  • Verify sellers;
  • Provide subscriptions and premium listings;
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity;
  • Investigate suspicious activity and policy violations;
  • Maintain platform security;
  • Handle support requests, reports, and complaints;
  • Manage ratings and reviews;
  • Improve and maintain the application;
  • Send transaction-related and important service notifications;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations; and
  • Protect BookSwap, its users, and third parties.

3. Payment Information

BookSwap may facilitate transaction payments through its designated payment arrangements, including its Easypaisa/Raast merchant payment arrangement.

For applicable transactions, the buyer's payment may initially be received by BookSwap and later settled to the seller after the applicable delivery and confirmation requirements have been satisfied.

BookSwap does not intend to collect or store complete card credentials where payment processing is performed by a third-party payment provider. Users should review the applicable terms and privacy practices of the payment provider used for a transaction.

4. Data Sharing

BookSwap does not sell users' personal information merely for the purpose of allowing unrelated third parties to market products or services to users.

BookSwap may share information where reasonably necessary to operate, secure, or protect the platform, including with:

  • Payment service providers;
  • Technology, hosting, and infrastructure providers;
  • Analytics and security providers;
  • Verification providers;
  • Courier or delivery-related services where necessary for an order;
  • Professional advisers;
  • Law enforcement or government authorities where legally required; and
  • Other parties where necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or unlawful activity.

5. Information Visible to Other Users

Certain information is intentionally displayed to other BookSwap users as part of the marketplace.

For sellers, this may include:

  • Display name;
  • Profile photograph;
  • Seller profile information;
  • Verification status;
  • Book listings;
  • Listing photographs;
  • Prices; and
  • Ratings and reviews.

Users should understand that information voluntarily included in public listings may be visible to other users.

6. Chat Privacy

BookSwap provides chat functionality so buyers and sellers can discuss books before a transaction. BookSwap may access or review communications where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, investigation of policy violations, responding to complaints, or protecting users, subject to applicable law.

Users should avoid sharing unnecessary sensitive personal information through chat.

7. Data Retention

BookSwap retains information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide its services, maintain transaction records, prevent fraud, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal or accounting requirements, and enforce its policies and legal rights.

When an account is deleted, BookSwap will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the active account where reasonably possible, subject to information that BookSwap is permitted or required to retain for legitimate purposes.

8. Account Deletion

Users may delete their account through Settings → Delete Account in the BookSwap application.

After account deletion, BookSwap may retain a limited internal deleted-account identifier and limited transaction, payment, security, fraud-prevention, or legally required records where reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes.

The retained deleted-account identifier is not used to maintain an active account or to continue ordinary personalized use of BookSwap. It may be used only for legitimate purposes such as security, fraud prevention, transaction records, legal compliance, or protecting BookSwap and its users.

Further details are provided in the Account Deletion Policy.

9. Active Transactions and Retained Records

If a user deletes an account while a transaction is active or incomplete, BookSwap may retain limited information necessary to complete, record, or resolve the transaction.

This may include order records, payment records, seller settlement information, delivery records, transaction references, and fraud-prevention information.

10. Account Security

BookSwap takes reasonable measures to protect user information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and should contact BookSwap promptly if they believe their account has been compromised.

11. Minor Users

BookSwap permits users aged 13 and above, subject to applicable law. Users between 13 and 17 should use BookSwap with appropriate parental or legal guardian involvement where required.

Parents or guardians who believe that a minor has provided information to BookSwap without appropriate permission may contact us.

12. Third-Party Services

BookSwap may use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, analytics, security, communication, verification, or other platform functions. Those services may process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BookSwap may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on the BookSwap website or application where appropriate, and the "Last Updated" date will indicate when it was most recently revised.
