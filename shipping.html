BookSwap supports transactions between buyers and sellers.

Delivery Methods

Depending on the agreement between the buyer and seller, books may be delivered by:

  • Local meetup
  • Courier service
  • Postal service

BookSwap does not directly handle shipping or delivery.

Shipping Charges

Shipping costs are determined by the seller or courier and should be clearly communicated before payment.

Delivery Time

Delivery times depend on:

  • Seller availability
  • Courier service
  • Buyer's location

BookSwap is not responsible for delays caused by shipping providers.

Lost or Damaged Shipments

If a shipment is lost or damaged:

  • Contact the seller immediately.
  • If a courier was used, file a claim with the courier company.
  • BookSwap may assist in resolving disputes but cannot guarantee reimbursement.
