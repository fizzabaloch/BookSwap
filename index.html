Problem

Students spend too much money every semester buying books.

Thousands of perfectly usable books sit unused, while students search across scattered channels and generic marketplaces that were never designed for the realities of book buying.

Students keep paying more than they should

Course materials are often purchased at full cost, even when a local used copy would work perfectly.

Books remain idle after one semester

Usable textbooks, novels, and study guides are left on shelves when they could keep circulating.

Nearby sellers are hard to find

Students lose time searching group chats and broad marketplaces instead of using a dedicated local layer.

Solution

Introduce BookSwap as one platform that makes local buying and selling simple.

BookSwap aligns affordability, accessibility, and convenience inside a clean mobile product experience that feels built for students from the start.

For buyers

  • Find books close to campus or in your neighborhood.
  • Compare condition and price without endless scrolling.
  • Send a purchase request in a focused flow.

For sellers

  • Upload once, describe the book clearly, and reach local buyers.
  • Answer questions through a purpose-built chat flow.
  • Complete the exchange faster with less back-and-forth.
