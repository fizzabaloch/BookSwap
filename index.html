FAQ

Frequently asked questions about BookSwap

Is BookSwap free?

Yes. BookSwap is a free Android app for buying and selling used books locally.

How do I sell my books?

Create a listing with photos, a price, and a short description, then wait for nearby buyers to connect.

Is payment handled inside the app?

Payment details can be handled based on your product plan, but the current app keeps the focus on local exchange.

Can I buy books from nearby sellers?

Yes. BookSwap is designed to surface nearby listings so you can buy locally with less friction.

Which devices are supported?

The MVP is built for Android devices, with this page acting as a promotional landing page.
