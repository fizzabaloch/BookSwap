BookSwap provides an online marketplace where users can buy and sell second-hand books locally.

Our Services Include

  • Buying used books

  • Creating user profiles

User Responsibilities

Users agree to:

  • Provide accurate listing information.
  • Upload genuine book photos.
  • Set fair prices.
  • Communicate respectfully with other users.
  • Avoid prohibited or illegal items.

BookSwap reserves the right to remove listings or suspend accounts that violate these guidelines.
